# Leaving the lab

Leaving well saves your future self, and the next person, a lot of time.
Start this checklist about a month before your last day.

## Hand off your work

- [ ] **Data and code** are on the lab server (not only on your laptop), in a project folder
  with a `README` that says what is where. See [Where data and files live](computing/data-and-servers.md).
- [ ] **GitHub repositories** for lab projects live in the
  [WinawerLab organization](https://github.com/WinawerLab), not only in your personal account.
- [ ] **Consent forms** are filed in the locked cabinet. See
  [Human subjects and the IRB](admin/human-subjects.md).
- [ ] **Unfinished papers.** Agree with Jon, in writing, on the plan and on authorship. See
  [Authorship and publishing](how-we-work/authorship.md).
- [ ] **Your wiki pages.** Find someone to take over each page you own, and put their GitHub
  name as `owner:`. Then set `current: false` for yourself in `people.yml`. If you forget, the
  weekly check will flag your pages.

## Keeping NYU access after you leave

What happens to your NetID depends on your role (department advice, December 2023):

- **Postdocs and staff:** access ends a few days after your appointment formally ends.
- **PhD students:** you keep your email after graduating, but access to some systems may end.

If you need NYU access to keep working on lab projects (for example the server or HPC), Jon can
sponsor an **affiliate** appointment through the department:

- **With a PhD:** a *Visiting Scholar* appointment, up to 12 months. Needs an application form,
  a certification form, your CV and a short description of the research.
- **Without a PhD:** a *Research Affiliate* appointment, one term at a time (it must be renewed
  each term). Needs an affiliate form, your CV, and proof you completed NYU's sexual
  harassment training.

Processing takes **3–4 weeks or more**, and Visiting Scholar applications need **1–2 months**
because of an extra review step. Applications that arrive after the start date may not be
reviewed. So ask Jon early. The forms come from the Assistant to the Chair of Psychology.
