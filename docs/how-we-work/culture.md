# Lab culture

This page should describe what it is like to work in this lab: what we value, and what we
expect of each other. It is a draft. We will write it together at a lab meeting.

!!! question "Questions for the lab to answer"
    Write the answers straight into this page. Short, honest answers are better than
    polished ones.

    1. **Working hours and availability.** Are there core hours? Is it fine to work from home?
       When should someone expect an answer to a Slack message or an email, and on weekends?
    2. **Lab meeting.** What is it for? How often does each person present? Is it fine to
       present unfinished work? (We hope so.)
    3. **Asking for help.** Who should you ask first: a labmate, Jon, or someone else? How long
       should you be stuck before you ask?
    4. **Giving feedback.** How do we give comments on each other's talks, posters and drafts?
    5. **Open science.** What do we share by default: code, data, preprints?
    6. **Wellbeing and time off.** How do you let people know you are taking time off?
       Where can you go if something is wrong and you do not want to raise it with Jon?
    7. **Disagreements.** What should you do if you disagree with Jon or a labmate about a
       project, authorship or credit?

## Ideas from other labs

Reading two or three other labs' documents is a fast way to start a discussion.
Note what you like and what does not fit us.
