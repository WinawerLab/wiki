# Who does what: the PI and trainees

This page should make clear what Jon is responsible for, and what each lab member is
responsible for. Clear roles prevent a lot of quiet frustration on both sides.
It is a draft. Jon writes the first version of his section; the lab writes the rest.

## What NYU already expects

Some of this is already written down by NYU. Our page adds what is specific to this lab.

- PhD students and their advisors sign the
  [GSAS mentor-mentee agreement](https://gsas.nyu.edu/content/dam/nyu-as/gsas/documents/policies-and-procedures-and-forms/GSAS_Mentor-Mentee_Agreement_Form.docx),
  based on the [GSAS guidelines on mentoring](https://gsas.nyu.edu/about-gsas/policies-and-procedures/gsas-guidelines-on-mentoring-doctoral-students.html).
- Psychology and CNS have also adopted a Code of Ethical Conduct for mentors and mentees.
  It is on the department's
  [Mentorship page](https://docs.google.com/document/d/10lLwqidkH1IzWf2P2ai5S3_l6X-kWPRw7zioupFhw48/edit)
  (NYU login needed).
