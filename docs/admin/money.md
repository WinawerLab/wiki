# Money and reimbursement

How to get paid back for lab expenses and travel. The rules below come from the Cognition
and Perception (C&P) program's email for academic year **2026–27** (sent October 2026).
The rules change most years, so if this page is more than a year old, check with the
program office before relying on it.

The official department policies live on
[PsychCentral → Policy](https://sites.google.com/nyu.edu/psychcentral/policy) (NYU login).
**If this page and PsychCentral disagree, PsychCentral wins.** Then please fix this page.

## Who to ask

- **C&P program office:** psych.request.cp@nyu.edu. Always include this address in
  requests, even when you write to a person.
- **Department finance** (which account to use, whether an expense is allowed):
  psych.request.finance@nyu.edu.
- **Jon:** before you spend lab money, ask Jon which account to use. Do not guess.

## The two deadlines that matter most

1. **Submit within 60 days** of the purchase, or of the end of a trip.
2. **After 90 days**, the payment counts as taxable income, and you need a "late
   justification". A lost receipt or a busy schedule does **not** count as a valid reason.

## What every request needs

- **Itemized receipts** (what was bought, not just the total).
- **Proof of payment** that shows the last 4 digits of the card. Emails from delivery apps
  usually show both. Save them as PDF.
- For expenses on a **grant**: one or two sentences on how the expense helps the grant's aims.

## How you submit depends on your role

| Role | How |
|---|---|
| Postdocs, research staff (anyone on payroll) | File an **Expense Report** in [Concur](https://us2.concursolutions.com/home) (NYU login). |
| Students | Do **not** use Concur. Email the program office **one PDF**: the expense cover sheet, then all receipts, then the card statements. You are paid through Albert. |

The department's Reimbursement Document Checklist and the cover sheets are linked from the
program office's email each year. Ask psych.request.cp@nyu.edu if you cannot find them.

## Travel to conferences

Postdocs, research staff and students need all of these:

1. **Pre-approval form.** Send it *before* you travel.
2. **Travel cover sheet.**
3. **Proof of attendance**, such as the program listing your talk or poster.
4. **Boarding passes** (screenshots are fine).
5. If you split costs with someone through Venmo or similar: a statement showing the payment.

Other rules:

- The department travel card can pay for your **flight, train or hotel**. You pay your own
  **registration and society membership**, then get reimbursed. Ask the program office early.
- Bookings should be economy class and refundable when possible.
- Do **not** book with rewards points or travel credits. Those cannot be reimbursed.
- **Society membership** usually cannot be charged to a federal grant, unless the membership is
  required to submit the abstract or attend. **Conference registration** can be.
- **Graduate students:** also look at the GSAS Dean's Student Travel Grant.

## Meals with guests (for example a visiting speaker)

NYU's business-meal limits (before tax and tip):

- Lunch up to **$35 per person**. Dinner up to **$75 per person**.
- Tip no more than **20%** of the subtotal.
- No more than 4 NYU hosts per guest (students do not count toward the 4).
- The **most senior person** present pays and submits the request.
- List every person who attended.

Food for lab social events usually **cannot** go on a federal grant. Ask Jon first.
