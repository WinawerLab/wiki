# Places and contacts

Tip for whoever edits this page: list **role addresses** (like psychit@nyu.edu) rather than
people's names when you can. People change jobs. Role addresses do not.

## Where we are

- **Building:** Meyer Hall, 6 Washington Place, New York, NY 10003.
- **Lab:** room 959, inside suite 957. You need key card access.
  To get it, send Jon your NYU ID number. He requests access through
  [Public Safety](https://www.nyu.edu/life/safety-health-wellness/public-safety/request-safety-services.html).
- **Jon's office:** 960.
- **Shared suite:** the Landy and Heeger labs share suite 957 with us.
- **Supply closet:** 8th floor, opposite the mailboxes (needs a key).

## Printing a poster

The Psychology poster printer is in the Psychology main office on the 6th floor (first room on
the left). Use the HP printing tool on the desktop and follow the instructions on the wall.

## Scheduling meetings at NYU

Google Calendar's "Find a time" usually works better than Doodle or when2meet, because
everyone at NYU shares calendars.
