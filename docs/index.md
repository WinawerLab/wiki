# Winawer Lab Wiki

This is the working handbook of the [Winawer Lab](https://wp.nyu.edu/winawerlab/) at NYU.
It is written by the lab, for the lab. Anyone can read it. Lab members can edit it.

The lab website is for the outside world (who we are, what we publish).
This wiki is for us: how things actually get done.

## Official sources come first

This wiki does not copy NYU or department policies. It links to them and adds what is
specific to our lab. The main official sources are:

- [PsychCentral](https://sites.google.com/nyu.edu/psychcentral/policy): Psychology department
  policies, forms and community pages (NYU login).
- [CBI](https://as.nyu.edu/research-centers/cbi.html): the MRI center's own policies and training.
- [NYU human subjects research](https://www.nyu.edu/research/resources-and-support-offices/getting-started-withyourresearch/human-subjects-research.html).

## This wiki is small on purpose

Our old wiki had 81 pages. By the end, most of them were out of date, and nobody could tell
which ones. So this wiki starts small and grows only when someone needs a page.

Every page has an **owner** and a **"last checked" date**. When a page goes too long without a
check, a yellow banner appears on it, and its owner gets a reminder.
[How it stays up to date](about/keeping-it-fresh.md) explains the details.

## You can help in under a minute

- **See something wrong?** Click the pencil icon (top right of every page) and fix it.
  [How to edit](about/how-to-edit.md) walks you through it.
- **Not sure what the right answer is?** Click "Report a problem" at the bottom of the page.
- **Wish a page existed?** [Ask for it](https://github.com/WinawerLab/wiki/issues/new?template=page-wanted.yml),
  or better, write a short first version yourself.
