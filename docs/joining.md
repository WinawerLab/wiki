# Joining the lab

Welcome! This page is a checklist for your first few weeks. If a step is wrong or missing,
please fix it. New lab members are the best people to keep this page accurate, because you
are the ones who just went through it.

## Week 1: access

- [ ] **NYU ID card.** Get it at the NYU Card Center
  ([how to get one](https://www.nyu.edu/nyucard/about/how.obtain.nyucard.html)).
  Students need a photo ID. Staff need an authorization form from HR.
- [ ] **Building and lab access.** Send Jon your NYU ID number. He requests key card access
  to Meyer Hall and suite 957. See [Places and contacts](admin/places-and-contacts.md).
- [ ] **Lab server and Google Drive.** Ask Jon. See [Where data and files live](computing/data-and-servers.md).
- [ ] **GitHub.** Make a [GitHub](https://github.com) account if you do not have one. Ask Jon to
  add you to the `winawerlab` team in the [WinawerLab organization](https://github.com/WinawerLab).
  This gives you the lab's code repositories and lets you edit this wiki.
- [ ] **Add yourself to `people.yml`** in this wiki. It is the list of current lab members, and
  the wiki uses it to find page owners. [How to edit](about/how-to-edit.md) shows you how; it
  makes a good first edit.
- [ ] **Lab calendar and mailing lists.** Ask Jon to add you.
- [ ] **Lab website.** Ask Jon to add you to the
  [People page](https://wp.nyu.edu/winawerlab/people/).

## Weeks 1–4: training

- [ ] **Human subjects training and IRB.** Needed before you test anyone. See
  [Human subjects and the IRB](admin/human-subjects.md).
- [ ] **MRI** (if you will scan). The Center for Brain Imaging (CBI) runs its own safety and
  operator training. Start with the [CBI website](https://as.nyu.edu/research-centers/cbi.html).
  Before operator training you need a funding source, IRB approval, and stimuli to test.
- [ ] **Money.** Read [Money and reimbursement](admin/money.md) before you buy anything or book travel.

## Get to know people

Meet each person in the lab one-on-one in your first month, for coffee or lunch. Ask what they
work on and what they wish they had known when they started. Then read
[Lab culture](how-we-work/culture.md) and [Who does what](how-we-work/roles.md).

## Finding housing

NYU lists off-campus housing on [NYU Home](https://home.nyu.edu) (Life tab; NYU login).
Lab members have also used the [Listings Project](https://www.listingsproject.com/) and
broker-free listing sites. Be careful with listings that send you to a broker.
If you found a good resource recently, add it here.
