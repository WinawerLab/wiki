# The old wiki

From about 2013 to 2025 the lab used a Confluence wiki hosted by NYU. NYU stopped supporting
Confluence, and the wiki was exported in July 2025.

We did **not** copy it over. Most of its 81 pages were out of date, and copying them would have
brought the same problem into the new wiki. Instead:

- The pages that were still in use (joining, leaving, IRB, money, server, publishing notes)
  were rewritten as short new pages here.
- The rest are listed in the
  [old wiki triage list](https://github.com/WinawerLab/wiki/blob/main/meta/old-wiki-triage.md).
  For each one, the lab decides: rewrite it here (with an owner), link to an official source
  instead, or drop it.
- The full export, and a Markdown copy of every old page, are kept on the lab server in the
  `Lab WIki` folder. They are not on GitHub, because some pages contain internal details.

If you need something that was on the old wiki, look there, then bring back only what is still
true, as a short new page with you as the owner.
